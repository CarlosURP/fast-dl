Part 1: 
Story - 
After being cut off from the rest of the world, your general has ordered you to get to, and kill, the empress alien.
Your team has been supplied with an APC, though it’s under fire, from the world's resistance group, that has commandeered a chopper. The resistance also has some of its militia armed with .50 Cal Chain Guns.

Objectives - 
* Get through the starting zone, and make your way to where the alien empress is held in one of the warehouses.
* Kill the empress and her minions for the FTL Chopper.


Part 2: 
Story - 
After losing the FTL Chopper, the general has found an access to Quam, through the end of the warehouse district. There is a subway in Quam, which can get you to a pick-up site.

Objectives - 
* Get through the rest of the warehouse district, and kill the NYPD. Once they have been killed, you will be transported to Quam, though in the wrong sub Way station, battle your way across Quam to the other sub way station.


Part 3: 
Story - 
You're now at the subway. Little help can be given in this area. The tracks have been blocked, and you need to find a new way to them.

Objectives - 
* Battle your way through to the offices, and take the vents to the track. Good luck.


Part 4:
Story - 
You have now found the subway, your commander says that there is a rescue operation waiting for you at the next station.
Hurry, but beware as the militia is heading there to try and stop you.

Objectives - 
* Get through the sub way system - no lights work in the system, so use your flashlight.
