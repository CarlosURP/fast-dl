++ Description
 A descent into the chaotic world of Chance "Cyraxx" Wilkins — a "man" living with his grandmother, whose house has become a maze of paranoia, decay, and fractured reality. What begins as a simple break-in spirals into a twisted journey through a collapsing home filled with hostile echoes, deranged mutations of Cyraxx himself, and the remnants of a mind split by obsession.
 Prank your way into Cyraxx's Attic as the environment shifts between grimy realism and nightmarish distortion.
Survive the house. Confront the nightmare in the attic. Escape the Den of Delusion… if the house lets you.
 
 -Collaborate with "Music Biz" Marty to take down the Plague at 339 Lloyd Street!
 -Communicate with Cyraxx via Laptop Transmissions to see what he's up to...
 -But beware, what REALLY lies in the attic above...?

*Based on True Stories*
